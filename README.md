\# PoultryStock: Inventory Management System for a Poultry Shop



\*\*Developer:\*\* Harold Suello

\*\*Course:\*\* Course 4: React Fundamentals with Laravel as Backend (Final Hands-On Exam, Mini Capstone)



\## Client

A small neighborhood poultry shop selling dressed chicken, eggs, live birds, feeds, and supplements, with one owner and two to three staff members.



\## Problem

Stock is tracked in a notebook and in the owner's memory. Spoilage, broken eggs, and bird mortality go unrecorded, supplier restock orders are untracked, and there is no history of who changed what. PoultryStock centralizes products, stock movements, suppliers, and purchase orders with low-stock alerts and an approval workflow.
